About Us

Flowers4U is located at Kabolonga high way, we offer a wide range of fresh flowers, plants, grave arrangements and ceramic giftware. All selected by our experienced buying team who keep up with the modern trends but still keep the old favourites in mind as well.

Fresh flowers

Bouquets, Arrangements, baskets, Funeral tributes, Wedding flowers, Etc…

Other products

Indoor Plants, Seasonal outdoor plants, Ceramic pots & vases, Grave ornaments, Balloons, silk flower arrangements

Delivery

Weddings, births, graduations and others
